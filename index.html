Beef Futures: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

The short version

Beef Futures has no accounts and no sign-in. We run no servers, and your game never sends anything to us. The only company that receives data from the app is Google, which serves the adverts that keep the game free.

What stays on your phone

Your farm, restaurant and progress are saved on your device. They never leave it, we cannot read them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

Beef Futures shows adverts through Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect:

DataWhy
Advertising identifier (IDFA / Android Advertising ID)To choose and measure adverts
IP address and approximate locationTo show adverts suited to your country and follow local rules
Device type, system version, languageTo display adverts correctly
Advert interactions, crash and performance dataTo measure adverts and prevent fraud

Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iPhone). The app asks before it may track you. If you choose not to allow it, you still see adverts but they are not personalised. Change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland. Google's consent form asks you before any personalised advert. You can change your answer in the game: Yard → Privacy choices for adverts.
  • Advertising ID. iOS and Android let you reset or turn off the advertising identifier in the system settings.
  • Delete everything. Uninstall the app. There is nothing stored on our side.

Children

Beef Futures is suitable for all ages but is a general-audience game, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
